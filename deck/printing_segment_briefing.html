04 · Observations, not conclusions

What the data shows, stated as observations

Structure

Printing's shrinking share of HP is a growth story elsewhere, not a decline story here. Personal Systems, likely AI PC demand, is outrunning it.

Composition

Supplies, not hardware, is carrying Printing's recent decline. 81% of the Q3 FY26 drop traces to Supplies alone.

Home Print

Consumer Printing units fell 9.0% while revenue fell only 1.8%, suggesting price or mix is offsetting volume.

Currency

A weakening dollar added roughly 1 to 2 points of growth to Printing across FY26, on top of the underlying trend.

Data integrity

The FY26 restatement moved a loss making business into Printing in the same move: revenue up, earnings down. The best evidence in this dataset that a single published number can hide two different stories depending on which release it came from.
